## 0. 相比他们原模板，我加固了三点

| # | 原模板的问题 | 本版改法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ① | 写「2px outline」但出图是 2K，2px 只占 0.1% | 改成「**≈ 长边 1.5–2%**」 | 描边粗细必须与尺寸无关，否则 512 图上看不见 |
| ② | 没约束"细刺/分离件" | 加「**单一闭合剪影、最细处 ≥ 宽 6%、无分离件**」 | 我们判定吃**一个**简单多边形；飘带、细天线会被抽稀抽掉或造成自交 |
| ③ | 白底 / 透明底混用（他们两批不一样） | 统一「**纯白 #FFFFFF 平底**」 | .NET 的 flood-fill 抠图对白底最稳；透明底常被模型画成棋盘格 |

Style rules that MUST be followed exactly: flat color fills only - absolutely NO gradients, NO soft airbrush shading, NO texture, NO gloss, NO specular highlight, NO reflection, NO drop shadow, NO ground plane, NO glow, NO 3D bevel. The object is built from exactly 3 to 4 flat tone steps of ONE color family: a lighter flat shape on the upper-left, the base tone in the middle, a darker flat shape on the lower-right and bottom, single light source from the top-left. Thin darker structural lines in the same hue family separate the parts; inner lines are visibly thinner than the outer outline. ONE dark outline of uniform thickness (about 1.5 to 2 percent of the object long side) in the same hue family wraps the entire silhouette. Muted slightly desaturated palette; EVERY color must stay below 78 percent brightness - no white, no near-white, no neon, no pure black. Background: flat pure white #FFFFFF, completely empty, no checkerboard pattern, no transparency grid, no vignette. The silhouette must be ONE closed shape with no detached pieces, no floating parts, and no razor-thin protrusions (the thinnest part must be at least 6 percent of the object width). No text, no watermark, no logo, no frame, no border, no perspective ground.
```

Composition: square 1:1 canvas, object centered horizontally, occupying 78 to 86 percent of the canvas height, generous margin. Crisp clean vector edges. Modern minimal game-art readability: the shape must be recognizable as a solid black silhouette at 96 pixels.
```

## 6. 我这边收到图后会做什么（也是你的验收口径）

| # | 检查 | 不过怎么办 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① | **白底可抠**（四角近纯白、主体不粘连边缘） | 退回重生成（或我手动补抠） |
| ② | **单一闭合剪影**，最细处 ≥ 宽 6% | 退回重生成（细刺会被抽稀成锯齿） |
| ③ | **亮度闸**：L > 78% 的像素 ≤ 1% | 我把超亮色往暗里拉（会略微偏色，需你确认） |
| ④ | **比例**：与目标长宽比误差 ≤ 15% | 我按目标比例重采样（会轻微变形） |
| ⑤ | **结构齐全**：§3 的必读结构 ≥ 5/6 | 缺关键结构就退回 |
| ⑥ | 描成多边形后：**顶点 12–80（D-119，挑战件与图鉴件同口径）**、`selfX=0`、`rot30<92%`、长边 1±2%、形心<0.05 | 我修形或退回 |
| ⑦ | **轮廓 vs 贴图**：长宽比一致到 0.01、面积占比合格（`python tools/aspect_audit.py`） | 重描（"只描最大连通分量"会丢书页/冰淇淋球/螃蟹腿，D-126 踩过） |
| ⑧ | **k 只做参考**：落在 0.013–0.080 即可，不再卡 0.020–0.047（D-135） | 带外才退回 |

## 8. 一件要先说清的事

这些图**不当判定资产**：进判定的永远是我们描出来的多边形（`pts` + `details`），原图留在 `02_美术源文件/gen_v11/`。
理由：图里带 C2PA `trainedAlgorithmicMedia` 标记（`Made with Google AI`），且判定必须吃可复现的多边形。
**但外观显示会用抠底贴图**（D-114/D-125：判定用多边形、显示用 256px 贴图；36 件已全部导出，外链不进包）。
